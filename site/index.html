From clip to review

A repeatable analysis workflow

Each step keeps the source video and the analysis connected, making it easier to review an observation against the actual movement.

01

Check the video

Record file identity, dimensions, frame rate, duration, and whether the clip can be read.

02

Mark key events

Use a human-reviewed release frame to define a useful window in each athlete’s own clip.

03

Track visible motion

Estimate pose landmarks over time and keep visibility information beside each point.

04

Review the evidence

Plot movement signals with the related frames and quality notes for coach and athlete review.

Design principles

Athlete-specific by default

  • ✓Review each athlete’s movement on its own terms; don’t rank athletes against one another.
  • ✓Only use landmarks that are visible and reliable for that athlete and camera view.
  • ✓Keep observations traceable to frames, timestamps, and data-quality notes.
What measurements mean

Support for review, not a verdict

Single-camera pose measurements are 2D image-plane estimates. They do not measure force, prove a technical error, or replace an athlete’s and coach’s judgement. Camera angle, occlusion, clothing, and landmark uncertainty all matter.
